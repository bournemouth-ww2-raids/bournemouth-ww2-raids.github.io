The documents linked to in the headings above are searchable PDFs, listing all 51 air raids suffered by the people of Bournemouth during the Second World War.

• Just click on the appropriate heading to download the associated document •

The first document lists details of the 172 civilian deaths which occurred in the town, as a direct result of the air raids.
The second lists the dates and times, the locations of the bombs dropped, and the casualty figures for each raid.
The data has been derived from Civil Defence records held in the Bournemouth Library Archives, and from the Official Register of Deaths.
You are welcome to download and use the documents as you wish, but the information obtained directly from the documents should not be used for commercial gain.
